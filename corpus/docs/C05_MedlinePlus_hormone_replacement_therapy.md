## Who should not take hormone replacement therapy

You should not use HRT if you:

- Think that you are pregnant
- Have problems with vaginal bleeding
- Have had certain kinds of cancers
- Have had a stroke or heart attack
- Have had blood clots
- Have liver disease

## Risks

For some women, hormone therapy may increase their chances of getting blood clots, heart attacks,
strokes, breast cancer, and gallbladder disease.
